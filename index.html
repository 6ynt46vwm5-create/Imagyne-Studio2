About us

Meet the
studio.

Co-founder

Sina Asli

Photographer · Visual Artist

Read Sina’s story +

Sina’s creative journey began with drawing and digital art, building a strong foundation in composition, colour theory, lighting and visual storytelling. His artistic background naturally evolved into photography and videography, where he combines technical precision with a creative eye to produce timeless imagery.

With years of experience using Procreate and Adobe Photoshop, he has developed advanced skills in digital illustration, photo retouching, image manipulation and creative editing. His experience in 3D modelling and visualisation further strengthened his understanding of perspective, depth and cinematic composition.

Today, Sina creates authentic visual stories across portraits, weddings, events and commercial content—with detail, creativity and a passion for images that last.

Co-founder

Vahid Adib

Photographer · Filmmaker · Creative Director

Read story +

With over a decade of professional experience in photography and filmmaking, Vahid has built a career around visual stories that blend creativity, emotion and technical excellence. His work spans portraits, weddings, commercial productions, branding campaigns, corporate events and creative content.

Passionate about the development of the creative industry, he has organised photography workshops, delivered professional training, mentored aspiring photographers and filmmakers, and coordinated creative events that connect artists in the community.

Known for meticulous detail and a cinematic approach, Vahid focuses on genuine emotions and timeless imagery. Every project is met with creativity, innovation and a commitment to exceptional visual experiences.
